    <div class="tooltiptext hide" id="tooltip_agreement_estimate_revision">
    <p>The Zacks Rank is comprised of four factors: Agreement, Magnitude, Upside and Surprise.</p>
    <p><strong>Agreement</strong> is the extent to which all earnings estimates are being revised in the same direction. The greater the percentage of estimates moving higher, the better the score will be for this component.</p>
    <p>For example: if there are 10 estimate revisions for a given period, and all were up, that would be a 100% positive agreement on the direction of estimate revisions. If 7 were up and 3 were down, that would be a 70% positive agreement. If 7 were down however, with only 3 being up, that would be a 70% negative agreement of the direction of estimate revisions.</p>
